Website version

The website version has no ads and no account, and we collect no personal information through it. Your game progress is stored only in your browser. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

TikTok version: information we process

  • Game progress: your best score, forged metals, daily streak, Melt items and sound and language settings. These are stored on your device through TikTok's storage.
  • TikTok login: the Game uses TikTok's silent login. If we run a login server, it receives an anonymous TikTok open_id. We do not request your name, photo, contacts or messages.
  • Advertising: rewarded video ads are served by TikTok. TikTok may process data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal data from those ads.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the Game, keep your progress, deliver the rewards you earn, and comply with the law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Game progress stays on your device until you remove the Game's data. You may contact us to ask for access to, or deletion of, any information we hold about you.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
