This Privacy Policy specifically explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you play Five Star Flight ("the Game") distributed via the TikTok Mini Game platform and mobile application platforms.

1. TikTok Mini Game: Information We Process

When you access Five Star Flight within TikTok:

  • Authentication via TikTok: The Game uses TikTok's native silent authentication (OpenID) to identify game sessions. Players do not create a separate third-party account with us. We do not access, collect, or store your real name, phone number, contacts, messages, or social graph.
  • Game Progress & Preferences: Your gameplay progress (including current flight stage, tips, unlocked seatbelts, flight stars, and audio preferences) is stored locally on your device or via TikTok's secure storage API.
  • Advertising & TikTok SDKs: In-game rewarded videos and interstitial advertisements are served directly by TikTok's advertising network. TikTok may process technical identifiers in accordance with its own Privacy Policy. We do not receive, store, or sell any personal identifying information from ad views.

2. How We Use Information

Any technical data or saved progress is processed solely to:

  • Provide and maintain gameplay functionality;
  • Save your levels, flight stars, and high scores;
  • Deliver in-game virtual rewards you choose to earn;
  • Ensure stability and security of the game runtime.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention and User Rights

Game data and local storage remain on your device and within your TikTok application cache until cleared. You may request data deletion or inquire about your data at any time by contacting our privacy officer at gm@technotouch.info.

4. Children's Privacy

Five Star Flight is a general-audience flight coordination game and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
