TikTok version: information we process

  • Game progress: your high score, coins, free continues and sound settings. These are stored on your device through TikTok's storage.
  • TikTok login: the Game uses TikTok's silent login. If we run a login server, it receives an anonymous TikTok open_id. We do not request your name, photo, contacts or messages.
  • Advertising: rewarded video ads and ads between rounds are served by TikTok. TikTok may process data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your personal data from those ads.

TapTap version

The Android version distributed on TapTap has no ads, no account and no in-app purchases. Your progress is stored only on your device, and we collect no personal information through the Game. TapTap may process data under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use this information only to run the Game, keep your progress, deliver the rewards you earn, and comply with the law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and your choices

Game progress stays on your device until you remove the Game's data. You may contact us to ask for access to, or deletion of, any information we hold about you.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes
